# ASME V&V 20 comparison

Source: ASME V&V 20-2009, *Standard for Verification and Validation in Computational
Fluid Dynamics and Heat Transfer*; see also Oberkampf & Roy (2010), ch. 12–13.

```
E      = S − D
δ_model ∈ [E − u_val, E + u_val]          (standard-uncertainty statement)
u_val  = sqrt( u_num^2 + u_input^2 + u_D^2 )
```

- `u_num`: from the GCI or another solution-verification estimate (divide the GCI by its
  factor of safety to get a standard uncertainty when combining).
- `u_input`: propagate uncertain inputs (inflow, material properties, geometry) by
  sensitivity coefficients or sampling.
- `u_D`: the experiment's reported uncertainty, including systematic parts.
- An expanded statement uses a coverage factor `k` and must say so. For example, `k = 2`
  gives about 95 %.

Report table: quantity, `S`, `D`, `E`, `u_num`, `u_input`, `u_D`, `u_val`, requirement,
verdict, sources.
