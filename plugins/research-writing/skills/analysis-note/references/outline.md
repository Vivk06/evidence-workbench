# Outline

1. Optional title page and contents, when the requested format needs them.
2. Answer: the result and how far it can be trusted, in a few sentences.
3. Problem: what is being asked, in one paragraph.
4. Setup: geometry, equations, parameters, with a figure.
5. Method: what was computed and how, with the evidence files.
6. Results: figures and tables, each followed by a hand check.
7. Interpretation: what the results mean and their limits.
8. Open points: what remains unknown and the check that would settle it.
